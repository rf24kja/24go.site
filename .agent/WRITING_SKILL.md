# Writing Skill: Human-Like Content for Reddit / Yandex Zen / Blog

## Anti-AI Writing Rules

### What NOT to do (AI patterns to ban)
- ❌ "In today's digital landscape..."
- ❌ "It's no secret that..."
- ❌ "Game-changer" / "Unlock the power" / "Leverage"
- ❌ "As we move forward..."
- ❌ "In conclusion..."
- ❌ "In the realm of..."
- ❌ "Let's dive in" / "Let's explore"
- ❌ "Comprehensive guide" / "Ultimate guide" / "Complete guide"
- ❌ "Whether you're a beginner or advanced..."
- ❌ "This article will cover..."
- ❌ "Without further ado"
- ❌ Passive voice everywhere
- ❌ Perfect grammar with no personality
- ❌ Numbered lists that read like a textbook
- ❌ Every paragraph starting with "Moreover" / "Furthermore" / "Additionally"
- ❌ No contractions (use "don't" not "do not", "it's" not "it is")
- ❌ No slang, no humor, no personality
- ❌ Perfect structure with no rough edges

### What TO do (human patterns)
- ✅ Start with a story or shocking statement
- ✅ Use first person: "I tried..." / "We tested..."
- ✅ Use contractions naturally
- ✅ Include specific numbers: "Sent 500 messages, got 23 replies"
- ✅ Admit mistakes: "I wasted $300 on this before figuring it out"
- ✅ Use short sentences mixed with long ones
- ✅ Include opinions: "Honestly, most outreach tools are overpriced"
- ✅ Reference real experiences (even if anonymized)
- ✅ Use bullet points for scanability
- ✅ Include "hot takes" that provoke discussion
- ✅ Write like you're talking to a friend at a coffee shop
- ✅ Use rhetorical questions
- ✅ End with a call to action or question
- ✅ Include disclaimers: "This is what worked for us, your mileage may vary"

## Reddit Post Format

### Structure
```
TL;DR at the top. 2-3 sentences max.

[Story/Hook] — 2-3 paragraphs, personal experience, specific numbers

[What we tried] — bullet points, what worked, what didn't

[Results] — actual metrics, tables if needed

[What we learned] — lessons, mistakes, recommendations

[Call to action] — "What's your experience? Anyone else tried this?"
```

### Rules
- TL;DR first (people skip if no TL;DR)
- 800-1500 words max
- Personal story + data
- Controversial opinion to spark discussion
- Ask a question at the end
- No links in the body (only in comments if needed)
- No selling — just share experience
- Use markdown: **bold** for emphasis, bullet points, tables

### Tone
- Conversational, like talking to a colleague
- Mix of humor and data
- Admit when something didn't work
- Be specific about failures
- "Here's what nobody tells you about X"

## Yandex Zen Article Format

### Structure
```
[Hook] — 1-2 sentences, emotional, shocking or curiosity-driven

[Personal story] — 3-5 paragraphs, first person, emotional

[Key insight] — what you learned, with specific numbers

[Practical tips] — 3-5 actionable bullets

[Conclusion] — short, emotional, with a question
```

### Rules
- 1000-2000 characters (short!)
- Personal story is EVERYTHING
- Emotional language: "I was shocked when..." / "It turned out..."
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- No tables (Zen doesn't render them well)
- Bullet points OK but keep them short
- Images/screenshots help a lot (mention them even if we can't add them)
- Title should be curiosity-driven: "How I lost $500 on Telegram ads (and what I learned)"

### Tone
- Like writing to a friend
- Emotional, not clinical
- Include feelings: "I was frustrated when..."
- Use "ты" (informal Russian) for Zen
- Conversational, not academic

## Blog Post Format (SEO-optimized)

### Structure
```
[H1] — keyword-rich title

[Intro] — 2-3 paragraphs, hook + what you'll learn

[H2 sections] — each with specific data, examples, actionable tips

[Tables] — comparison tables, cost breakdowns, metrics

[FAQ] — common questions with short answers

[Conclusion] — summary + CTA
```

### Rules
- 3000-5000 characters
- Include tables with data
- Include specific numbers and percentages
- Reference sources where possible
- Use H2/H3 hierarchy
- Include meta description
- Keywords in first paragraph
- Images/diagrams help (mention in alt text)

## Writing Checklist (use for every article)

- [ ] Does it start with a story or hook?
- [ ] Is there a specific number in the first 3 sentences?
- [ ] Is it written in first person?
- [ ] Are there contractions?
- [ ] Is there a controversial opinion?
- [ ] Are there bullet points or tables?
- [ ] Is it under 5000 chars (Reddit) or 2000 chars (Zen)?
- [ ] Does it end with a question or CTA?
- [ ] Does it contain ZERO AI patterns from the ban list?
- [ ] Would you actually read this if you saw it on Reddit/Zen?

## Content Transformation Examples

### Before (AI-style)
```
In today's digital landscape, lead generation has become increasingly 
important for businesses. This comprehensive guide will explore the 
various strategies and techniques that can help you optimize your 
lead generation efforts in Telegram.
```

### After (Human-style)
```
Last month I spent $400 on Telegram outreach and got exactly 2 
meetings. Both were tire-kickers. Here's what I should have done 
instead (and what actually works now).
```

### Before (AI-style)
```
There are several key metrics that businesses should track when 
measuring the success of their lead generation campaigns, including 
response rates, conversion rates, and cost per lead.
```

### After (Human-style)
```
Here's the thing nobody tells you about lead gen metrics: response 
rate means nothing if nobody's buying. I tracked 12 metrics for 6 
months. Only 3 actually predicted revenue. The rest was vanity.
```

## Language Rules (Russian)

### Zen (informal)
- Use "ты" not "вы"
- Short sentences
- Emotional words: "шокирован", "оказалось", "в итоге"
- Colloquial: "короче", "честно", "на самом деле"

### Blog (neutral)
- Use "вы" (formal)
- Professional but not dry
- Include specific numbers
- Tables for comparisons

### Reddit (English)
- Casual, conversational
- First person singular
- Contractions everywhere
- Rhetorical questions
